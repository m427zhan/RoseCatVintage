Our Story

Rosecat Vintage started with a love of well-made things that have a story to tell. We search estate sales, flea markets and forgotten closets for pieces worth a second life.

Every item is cleaned, checked and photographed with care, so you know exactly what you're getting.

From everyday wear to one-of-a-kind finds, we hope you discover something you'll treasure.
