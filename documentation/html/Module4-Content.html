Module 4 — Content

Goal: Write the text and prepare the photos.

4.1 Text

Section Content Done?
Hero Tagline (one line) ☐
About 2–3 short paragraphs: who you are, what you sell, what makes it special ☐
Shop One line per marketplace (e.g. "Clothing & accessories on Etsy") ☐
Contact Email, response time, social handles ☐

Tips: keep it short and friendly, and write in the brand's voice.

4.2 Photos

  • Logo (PNG or SVG, transparent background)
  • One hero / banner image
  • 4–8 gallery photos of favorite pieces
  • Consistent lighting and background
  • Resized (max ~1600px wide) and compressed (e.g. with squoosh.app)
  • Descriptive file names (1970s-floral-dress.webp)

4.3 Search & Sharing Basics

  • Page title: e.g. "Rosecat Vintage — Curated Vintage Clothing & Finds"
  • Meta description (≤ 160 characters)
  • Social share image (Open Graph tag) so links look good on Instagram and Facebook

✅ Module 4 Checklist

  • All text written and proofread
  • Photos prepared and added
  • Title, description and share image set
