# Module 1 — Planning

**Goal:** Decide what the Rosecat Vintage website needs to say and where it sends visitors.

The site is a simple display website. It shows the brand and sends shoppers to the marketplaces where the items are actually sold. It has no checkout, no database and no customer accounts.

---

## 1.1 Purpose

> _e.g. "Introduce Rosecat Vintage and send visitors to our eBay, Etsy and other shops."_

What should a visitor do after landing on the site?

1. Get a feel for the brand
2. Click through to a marketplace shop
3. Follow on social media or get in touch

## 1.3 Site Structure

A single page with sections is the simplest option and is recommended:

```
index.html
├── Hero        (logo, tagline, "Shop Now" buttons)
├── About       (short brand story)
├── Shop        (cards linking to eBay, Etsy, etc.)
├── Gallery     (a few featured photos, optional)
└── Contact     (email + social links)
```

You can add separate pages later if the site grows.
