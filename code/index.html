Take a gaslit stroll down our street of little shops: fine jewellery, charms, home décor, clothing, hats & gloves, and curiosities. Step inside any door, and the shopkeeper will be glad to see you.

How to stroll

  • Look: drag on the screen. Q / E also turn.
  • Walk: hold ▲ ▼ ◀ ▶, or W A S D / arrow keys.
  • Enter a shop: walk up to its door and choose “Yes”.
  • Inside: click any item to pick it up. “Put it back” or Esc to return it.
  • Staff: click anyone in the shop to chat. Near the stairs, they will offer to show you up, or just walk up yourself.
  • Subtitles: click them (or press Space) to move on.
  • Leave: walk back to the door, or press “Leave the shop”.
